# Platform terms of service

Version: 1.0-draft
Date: 2026-10-21
Status: draft for counsel review (RSK-027). Not in force until the platform operator publishes it with a launch date.
Specification: GOV-026. Applies to hosted deployments only. Self-hosted operators write their own equivalents.

## Scope

These terms govern the relationship between the platform operator and a network (its administrators), not the rules a
network sets for its own members. A network's rules are its own policy and terms (GOV-001 to GOV-009).

## What the operator provides

Hosting of the Circula software for the network, backups, security updates, and support for the software and the
deployment (support boundaries, GOV-027).

## What the network is responsible for

Its rules, its members, its trades and balances, and disputes between members. The operator does not decide them and
does not take part in them (GOV-024).

## Changes to these terms

A material change is announced to network administrators at least 14 days before it takes effect (DEC-025) and the
version and date above change. A change that is not adverse may take effect on publication.

## Open points for counsel

Liability, governing law, fees and termination are not drafted here. They belong in the written hosting agreement.
