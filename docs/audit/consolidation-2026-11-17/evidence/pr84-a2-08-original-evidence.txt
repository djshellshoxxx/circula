132: - Where: `docs/api/openapi.yaml`; `codeStatus` (`internal/api/respond.go:103-111`) maps `network-frozen` to 423 and busy to 503 for any operation; the edge limiter (`middleware.go:52-57`) and the principal limiter (`route.go:73-76`) return 429 on any route.
