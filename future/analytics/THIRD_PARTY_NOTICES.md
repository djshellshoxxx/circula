# Analytics third-party notices

No third-party source or assets are vendored, and `go.mod` declares no external dependencies. The package uses the Go standard library supplied by the build toolchain. Go's BSD-style license and third-party toolchain notices remain in the toolchain distribution; this repository does not copy or redistribute that distribution in this feature.

Chart.js, @kurkle/color, Gonum and go-echarts were researched but are not shipped. Their upstream revisions and license references are retained in UPSTREAM-RESEARCH.md. Do not copy or bundle those assets without their notices, pinned integrity and an updated dependency review.
