# Getting Started

**Status: Framework; no runnable Circula application is currently available.**

This page will become the verified setup guide after there is a release. For now it lists decisions a prospective network and contributor should make before implementation or pilot use.

## For a prospective network

- Define the community and intended exchange needs.
- Decide who can join and who administers membership.
- Define the accounting unit and explain that it does not promise cash redemption.
- Set initial credit limits, acceptance steps, dispute process, privacy visibility, and closure/export process.
- Identify operational support, backups, and succession responsibilities.
- Resolve the open questions in [Assumptions, Risks, Decisions, Roadmap](../docs/specs/13-decisions-risks-roadmap.md).

## For members

Before joining a future network, read its participation and privacy policies, understand positive and negative balances, and know how to propose, accept, decline, or dispute a trade.

## For contributors

Review the specifications index, domain model, and quality strategy. Implementation setup steps will be added after the technology stack and contribution workflow are selected.
