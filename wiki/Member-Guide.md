# Member Guide

**Status: Framework; workflows are planned, not yet available in an application.**

## Intended member tasks

1. Review a network’s rules and request or accept membership.
2. Create, update, pause, or close an offer or request.
3. Propose an exchange with a description and amount.
4. Review the counterparty, amount, and balance effects before accepting.
5. Review posted balance, available credit, pending proposals, and history.
6. Raise a dispute and follow its status.

## Member rights and responsibilities to document

Each network should explain its unit, credit limit, listing visibility, transaction acceptance, member data access, dispute and appeal process, and departure rules. Specific workflows depend on selected network policy.

When an application exists, replace this outline with version-verified instructions, screenshots where useful, and recovery guidance for errors.
