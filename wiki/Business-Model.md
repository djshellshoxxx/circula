# Business Model

**Status: Exploration; no model selected.**

Circula could pursue commercial success by selling reliable operations, convenience, and expertise around community-controlled software. Candidate revenue paths include hosted network subscriptions, managed self-hosted deployments, paid support and recovery services, onboarding, training, integrations, and later federation support.

The proposed trust direction is to keep core accounting behavior inspectable and portable, charge for optional services, and avoid hidden deductions from mutual-credit balances or monetizing member transaction data. Pricing, licensing, company structure, and paid feature boundaries are unresolved.

See [business model options](../docs/brainstorming/business-model-options.md). This is business exploration, not a financial forecast.
