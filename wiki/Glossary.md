# Glossary

**Status: Draft; domain-spec definitions are authoritative.**

- **Account:** a member’s ledger position within one network.
- **Adjustment:** an authorized accounting event that applies or corrects a decision without erasing prior entries.
- **Audit event:** an attributable record of an action or state change.
- **Balance:** the signed sum of posted entries for an account.
- **Credit limit:** policy cap on the permitted negative balance.
- **Credit unit:** the network-defined accounting unit; not presumed to be currency or a token.
- **Dispute:** a case challenging a transaction or its fulfillment.
- **Ledger entry:** a signed change to an account linked to a transaction or adjustment.
- **Member:** a person or organization admitted to a network.
- **Network:** a separately governed group using one unit and policy set.
- **Offer/request:** a listing of value available or sought; not a commitment to transact.
- **Transaction:** a proposed exchange between accounts with a positive amount and description.
- **Reversal:** a linked compensating entry; it preserves the original posting in history.

See the [domain model](../docs/specs/03-domain-model.md) for the shared vocabulary.
