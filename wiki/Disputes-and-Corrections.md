# Disputes and Corrections

**Status: Draft framework based on current specifications.**

A dispute is a formal challenge linked to a transaction. The network’s policy defines evidence, timelines, reviewer authority, recusal, and appeal. The system should record who opened the case, its state, assigned reviewer, access to evidence, decision, and any resulting action.

A dispute does not silently change a posted balance. If the network authorizes a correction, the ledger receives a linked reversal or adjustment with a reason, actor, time, and reference to the original event.

## Page sections to complete when implementation is available

- Open and view a dispute
- Provide or access evidence
- Reviewer assignment and recusal
- Record a decision or appeal
- Apply an authorized reversal or adjustment
- Troubleshoot access and notification issues
