# Roadmap and Decisions

**Status: Draft; this is not a delivery schedule.**

## Current stage

Foundational specifications and wiki framework are being prepared. No application features or release dates are claimed.

## Dependency sequence

1. Resolve deployment, admission, pending-credit, account-topology, and retention decisions.
2. Confirm the accounting lifecycle and ledger invariants.
3. Select implementation architecture, identity, API, and storage approaches.
4. Build the MVP with invariant-focused tests.
5. Validate accessibility, exports, backups, and recovery in a pilot.
6. Consider future ideas only after member need and operational impact are reviewed.

## Decision tracking

The [decision register](../docs/specs/13-decisions-risks-roadmap.md) is authoritative. Accepted choices should be recorded with context, alternatives, consequences, owner, and date. This page should summarize accepted decisions and their status without duplicating the full record.
