# Governance

**Status: Draft framework. Network policy is determined by each network.**

Circula is intended to enforce recorded policy and accounting invariants, not choose the community’s values. Each network should publish current rules for participation, credit limits, transaction acceptance, disputes, visibility, administrator authority, and closure.

## Governance topics

- How rules are proposed, announced, approved, and changed
- Which decisions administrators may make and which require member approval
- Reviewer conflicts, challenges, and appeals
- Administrator succession and recovery
- Negative-balance recovery and service suspension
- Network closure, outstanding balances, disputes, and data export

Platform-wide invariants include balanced postings, explicit corrections, authorization checks, and auditability. See the [governance specification](../docs/specs/10-governance-operations.md) and [[Roadmap and Decisions]].
