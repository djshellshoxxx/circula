# FAQ

**Status: Draft; answers reflect current project intent.**

### Is Circula a cryptocurrency?

No cryptocurrency or blockchain is required by the mutual-credit model. “Crypto” may also mean cryptographic tools such as signatures or verifiable records; those are future options, not selected architecture. See [[Crypto and Decentralization]].

### Do Circula credits have a cash value?

The project does not promise monetary value, acceptance, or redemption. Each network defines an accounting unit and its rules.

### Is there a working application?

Not yet. The repository is currently in documentation and design. Wiki workflow pages are planned behavior unless explicitly marked Verified for a release.

### Who controls network rules?

Each network is intended to govern its own participation, limits, and dispute process within platform-wide accounting and security invariants.

### How could Circula make money?

Potential options include hosting, managed operations, support, onboarding, integrations, and optional administrative tools. No business model has been selected. See [[Business Model]].

### What is the roadmap?

Open decisions and dependencies are tracked in the [decision register](../docs/specs/13-decisions-risks-roadmap.md) and summarized in [[Roadmap and Decisions]].
