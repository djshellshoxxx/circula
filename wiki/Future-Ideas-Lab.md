# Future Ideas Lab

**Status: Brainstorming only. No idea below is an approved feature or roadmap commitment.**

The idea lab explores possible social impact while keeping the baseline product small and testable. Concepts should be judged by member need, voluntary participation, accounting integrity, privacy, governance, feasibility, and exit rights.

- [Disruptive future directions](../docs/brainstorming/disruptive-future-ideas.md): federation, mutual aid, portable credentials, community governance, resilience measures, and other possibilities.
- [Business model options](../docs/brainstorming/business-model-options.md): possible commercial services and value capture.
- [Crypto and crypto-libertarian design](../docs/brainstorming/crypto-and-crypto-libertarian-design.md): distinction between cryptography, cryptocurrency, blockchain, and decentralization choices.

Before an idea moves to a specification, state its user problem, smallest pilot, governance model, failure modes, and open legal or operational questions.
